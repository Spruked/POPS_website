# P.O.P.S. launch demonstrations and recruitment

Prepared October 21, 2026. Concepts only; nothing published or scheduled.

## Message

Presence matters. A factual record deserves context. P.O.P.S. gives fathers a practical way to organize parenting-time notes and supporting material for review. Preserve. Protect. Prove.

Connect each capability to a concrete task. Do not say that a hash proves a real-world event, that a recorded location independently confirms presence, or that a packet guarantees admissibility or a result.

## TikTok Live: From a missed exchange to an organized record

Suggested running order, adjustable to the verified build:

1. **The reason (one minute).** Explain the difficulty of reconstructing an exchange from memory, messages, and separate files. Speak about accountability and preparation rather than attacking another parent.
2. **Name the build and the boundaries (one minute).** Windows release/build on screen, fictional-data notice, available tasks, and known limitations. No launch date is implied.
3. **Create the event (three minutes).** In a fictional Alex example, enter the scheduled exchange and what was personally observed. Distinguish uncertainty and notes from source facts.
4. **Connect the supporting record (three minutes).** Associate a harmless example message or receipt. Show source context and explain originals versus screenshots/copies.
5. **Find the chronology (two minutes).** Return to the same event in the timeline and show how connected material reduces searching.
6. **Demonstrate integrity only if verified (two minutes).** Explain a SHA-256 reference comparison. If a feature cannot be demonstrated in the build, state that directly.
7. **Show an actual output only if verified (two minutes).** Open the report/package and identify what it contains. Otherwise explain that export validation remains outstanding.
8. **Next steps and questions (three minutes).** Link Windows information, beta interest, and first-100 program status separately. State that checkout is unavailable. Do not accept case files or discuss identifiable family matters in chat.

Preparation: verify the build and workflows, use fictional inputs, hide unrelated desktop windows/notifications, prepare readable captions, and record the session for an edited replay. Do not portray representative website artwork as an actual application capture. Android and sync should have their own recording only after testing.

## Short beta recruitment clips

- **Clip A: Can you find the event again?** Show one harmless example from creation to chronology. Explain the goal of 50 active participants, the invitation process, and the platform preference field.
- **Clip B: Useful feedback beats “it broke.”** Demonstrate a sanitized report: build, OS, steps, expected and actual behavior. Point to /support and choose Beta feedback.
- **Clip C: A tester is not automatically a customer.** Explain that interest is not admission or a license. Participation/access terms accompany invitations. Optional updates are separate from beta consent.

Suggested CTA: “Help test a practical workflow. Read the expectations and express interest at the P.O.P.S. beta page.” Do not claim 50 remaining places, free access, or a fixed test period without approval.

## First-100 paid customers

Landing destination: /early-adopters, then /access and /cart. The current website can save a selection but cannot accept payment or issue qualification. Do not call an account signup, saved cart or beta application a reservation.

Before publishing a special offer, approve the qualifying purchase, actual benefit, license scope, refund/reversal handling, availability, and reliable accounting for completed payments. Existing published prices are not a new discount. No lifetime upgrades or Android entitlement is implied.

## Possible limited software-license giveaway

Status: not approved or active. Do not announce a quantity, dates, platform entitlement, selection mechanism, or odds before terms are approved.

Decision sheet: number of licenses; Windows build and included rights; eligibility and territories; entry method; timing and time zone; selection and notification; treatment of an unreachable recipient; relationship to beta participation; privacy/retention; applicable platform/promotion terms. Obtain appropriate review before publication.

Entry must not require case disclosures or a positive review. Keep it separate from Noted Counsel recognition. Never suggest that a giveaway predicts a legal result.
